Privacy Policy

1. Scope and operator

This Privacy Policy explains how {{LEGAL_ENTITY}} (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use {{APP_NAME}}, including information received through official third-party APIs and OAuth authorization. Operator location: {{COUNTRY_OR_STATE}}.

2. Information we access or collect

Depending on the features you use and permissions you grant, we may process:

  • Account information: account identifiers, display name, profile image and contact information you directly provide.
  • OAuth data: provider identifiers, granted scopes, access tokens, refresh tokens and authorization status where required to maintain a connection.
  • Platform API data: profile, channel, content/video metadata, publishing status, statistics or other data permitted by authorized scopes.
  • Technical data: IP address, device/browser details, security events and server logs used for operation, security and troubleshooting.

We do not request or store your TikTok, Google or YouTube account password.

You should also review the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

TikTok

  • provide user-facing features you request;
  • connect and maintain authorized third-party accounts;

  • perform actions that you explicitly request and approve;
  • protect users, prevent abuse and diagnose failures;

Limited information may be processed by service providers that operate hosting, infrastructure, security, logging or support functions on our behalf.
